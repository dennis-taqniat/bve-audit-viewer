TDC Browser Visual Editor

A Chrome extension from TDC for front-end developers and designers.

TDC Browser Visual Editor lets you change your own React and Vite app visually, in the browser, and get each change as a small, reviewed edit to your source files.

What it does

  • Pick an element on your local page and see its styles and where it comes from in your code.
  • Change spacing, colour, type and layout, and see the result on the page straight away.
  • Review the exact source diff first. Nothing is written to your files until you approve it.
  • Undo, redo and revert earlier changes. Every written change is recorded in an audit log.

Who it is for

Teams where designers adjust a running app and a developer owns the repository. The audit log lets a reviewer see who changed what, in which files, and what was refused.

Where it runs

Only on pages served from your own computer (localhost, 127.0.0.1). It edits only the project folder you choose. You sign in with Google so changes are recorded under your account.
